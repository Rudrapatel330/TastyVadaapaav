Our Story

About Us

From a Humble Cart to Vadodara's Favorite

It started in 2005 with a single cart, a secret family recipe, and a burning passion for the perfect Vadapaav. Every morning, our potatoes are hand-picked, our spices freshly ground, and our bread baked to golden perfection.

What makes us different? It's not just food — it's an emotion. Each bite carries the warmth of generations of culinary wisdom, the zing of our legendary dry garlic chutney, and the love of Vadodara.
